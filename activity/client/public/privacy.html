Privacy Policy

QapBot (Discord application "Qaps-CoC-Bot") · Last updated: 23 September 2026

This policy explains what data QapBot collects, why, and what rights you have. QapBot is a free, non-commercial Discord bot for Clash of Clans clans. It is run by a private individual.

1. Who is responsible

The controller responsible for data processing is the operator of QapBot, reachable on Discord as qaplop. For any privacy request, send a direct message to that Discord account.

2. What data we process

  • Discord identifiers: your Discord user ID and display name, the IDs of servers, channels, roles and messages the bot works with, and server settings made by server admins.
  • Linked Clash of Clans accounts: player tags you link to your Discord account, together with player name, Town Hall level, current clan, and whether the link was verified. Player tags you choose to watch ("buddies") are stored the same way.
  • Your bot preferences: language, war/raid reminder settings, notification subscriptions, and Clan War League sign-up choices (opt-in/opt-out, preferred league).
  • Clash of Clans game data: clan, war, Clan War League and Clan Capital data (attacks, stars, rosters, member lists) fetched from Supercell's public Clash of Clans API. This data is public in the game and is kept as history for statistics and leaderboards, including for players who have since left a clan.
  • Bug reports and feature requests you submit through the bot, including any text and attachments, together with your Discord user ID and name.
  • Technical logs: the bot writes operational logs (commands run, errors) that may include Discord IDs and names.

QapBot does not read or store the content of normal chat messages, and it does not collect payment data, email addresses, or IP addresses.

3. Why we process it (purpose and legal basis)

The data is used only to provide the bot's features: linking your Discord and game accounts, war and raid reminders, clan roles, Clan War League planning, statistics and leaderboards, and fixing bugs. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (providing the service you or your server requested) and Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (the legitimate interest of the clan communities in running their clans and keeping war history). Data is never sold, used for advertising, or shared for any other purpose.

4. Discord Activity sign-in

When you open the bot's Discord Activity, you sign in with Discord (OAuth2). The Activity's backend runs on Cloudflare and only uses your access token to confirm who you are with Discord. It does not store the token.

5. Where the data is stored and who receives it

  • Bot data is stored on privately operated hardware in Germany.
  • Discord processes everything the bot posts or receives, under Discord's Privacy Policy.
  • Supercell provides the game data through its public API. The bot sends player and clan tags to that API to look them up.
  • Cloudflare hosts the Discord Activity (web frontend and sign-in backend).

6. How long data is kept

  • Account links, buddies and preferences are kept until you remove them or ask for deletion.
  • Server settings are kept while the bot is in the server, and are deleted on request.
  • Game history (war, CWL and raid results) is kept for as long as the bot provides statistics, because it is the basis for long-term leaderboards.
  • Logs are kept only as long as needed for operating and debugging the bot.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access your data, to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict or object to its processing, and to receive it in a portable format. To use any of these rights, message qaplop on Discord. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.

8. Children

QapBot is only available through Discord, which requires users to meet its minimum age. The bot does not knowingly collect any extra data about children.

9. Changes

This policy may be updated when the bot changes. The date at the top shows the latest version.
